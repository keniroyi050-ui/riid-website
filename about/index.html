About RIID

Small studio.
Serious standards.

RIID exists to help good businesses stop looking ordinary online.

We care about the details customers notice — and the ones they don’t.

A website can be the first office, showroom, salesperson or proof of credibility a customer sees. RIID treats it that way.

Our approach is intentionally focused: understand the business, create a distinct visual direction, build the experience properly, and make the next action obvious.

Principles

How we think about the work.

01

Different business, different website.

We do not believe twelve industries should look like twelve recoloured versions of the same layout.

02

Clarity beats decoration.

Beautiful work still has to help people understand, trust and act.

03

Nigerian context matters.

The language, imagery, devices, customer habits and conversion paths should feel relevant here.

04

Premium does not mean noisy.

Confidence comes from typography, hierarchy, photography, motion and restraint — not from adding more effects.
